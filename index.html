Four ways to play

Pick your chaos

Online with friends or solo against bots - bots fill every empty slot, so matches start fast.

Battle Royale on Stick Island

Jump from the plane and land anywhere on a 1.2 km island. Solo, Duo or Squad - up to 60 players.

  • SNIPER TOWERS: a high-rise city with elevators, sniper roofs and ziplines
  • Harbour, neon market, prison rock, wonder park with ferris wheel, junkyard, farm
  • Loot glows by rarity, supply crates, death boxes, knocks and revives
  • A shrinking zone herds everyone together

Tactical 5v5

Plant or defuse the bomb on 12 hand-built maps - with a buy menu, an economy and a full utility set.

  • Recoil patterns, headshots and wallbangs
  • Smokes, flashes, frags and molotovs
  • A kill feed that tells the story: no-scopes, shots through smoke, airborne kills, roadkills

FUN MODES

ONLY KNIFE, ONLY UTILITY, ONLY PISTOL, ONLY SNIPER and ONLY BAT - five quick party modes on their own crazy maps, from a glass hive to a dirty neon back alley.

  • The baseball bat sends enemies flying into walls with a home run
  • Own queue online

Campaign & arenas

10 missions from an office tower to a boss fight, plus 3 arenas - against enemies that flank, take cover and call for help.

  • Play it alone, completely offline
  • Adjustable difficulty

A comic you can shoot through

Break everything

Almost nothing in STICKSHOT is indestructible. The fight reshapes the map - and the map reshapes the fight.

Glass

Shattering world

Shoot out lights, trigger alarms, burst pipes and shatter glass floors and facades.

Walls

Holes and collapse

Bullets punch holes through thin walls, rockets and grenades blast rooms open - floors, stairs and roofs come down.

Bodies

Real ragdolls

Stick fighters fly, tumble and fall with real physics. Wounds and splatter in RED or INK style - blood and gore can be reduced or turned off.

Vehicles & ziplines

Drive buggies, pickups and motorbikes - your squad rides along and shoots from the seats. Wrecks burn and explode, and you can smash through fences and thin walls. Zip between skyscrapers or take the glass lift to the sky deck.

  • Fell trees and take cover behind them

Weapons that hit hard

13 guns. Lots of style.

Firearms

From pistol to minigun - including an UZI, an M4, a sawed-off double barrel and an auto shotgun. Detailed comic models with their own draw, reload and inspect animations.

Melee

The knife, a katana with a charged super slash and a baseball bat whose home run launches enemies into walls. Pull the pin, light the molotov - every hand animation is hand-tuned.

Fair cosmetics

Earn coins by playing. Spin the LUCKY SPIN or open the weekly crate for weapon skins, costumes, knives and dances. Skins change the look, never the stats. Nothing is sold for real money.

Made for you

Controls

Fully rebindable keyboard and mouse. Controller support for gameplay and menus, with aim assist and button remapping.

10 languages

Online

Matchmaking on Steam with voice and text chat. Bots fill empty slots.

Blood & gore

The comic style is rough but adjustable: blood in RED or INK style, gore can be turned off. Made for adults.
