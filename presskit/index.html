One-liner

A loud neon-comic stickman shooter with a 60-player Battle Royale, tactical 5v5 and destruction everywhere.

Short (under 300 characters)

A loud neon-comic stickman shooter. Drop into a 60-player Battle Royale on a destructible island - drive buggies, zip between skyscrapers, slice with a katana - plant the bomb in tactical 5v5 or fight a 10-mission campaign. Real ragdolls, shattering glass, no pay-to-win.

About the game

STICKSHOT: Comic War is a fast first-person shooter drawn like a comic book: bold colours, thick ink lines and stick fighters who fly, tumble and ragdoll when they get hit. Every fight looks like a panel from a comic - and almost everything in it can break.

Up to 60 players drop onto STICK ISLAND, a 1.2 km island with a high-rise city (SNIPER TOWERS) full of elevators and rooftop ziplines, a harbour, a neon market, a wonder park with a ferris wheel and more. Buggies, pickups and motorbikes carry whole squads, wrecks burn and explode, and bullets punch holes through thin walls. Bots fill every empty slot, so matches start fast.

Prefer tactics? Plant or defuse the bomb on 12 hand-built 5v5 maps with a buy menu, economy and a full utility set. Want a break? Try ONLY BAT or ONLY KNIFE, or play the 10-mission campaign alone.

Cosmetics are earned by playing - coins, a LUCKY SPIN and a weekly crate - and never change the stats. Nothing is sold for real money.

About Millennial Game Dreams

Millennial Game Dreams is an independent game developer. STICKSHOT: Comic War is built around one idea: a shooter that looks like a comic book and lets you break everything - without ever selling an advantage.

Key features

Battle Royale

  • Up to 60 players - Solo, Duo or Squad
  • 1.2 km STICK ISLAND with SNIPER TOWERS, harbour, neon market, prison rock, wonder park, junkyard, farm
  • Loot by rarity, supply crates, death boxes, knocks and revives, shrinking zone
  • Drivable buggies, pickups and motorbikes; ziplines; glass lift

Tactical 5v5

  • Plant / defuse on 12 hand-built maps
  • Buy menu, economy, smokes, flashes, frags, molotovs
  • Recoil patterns, headshots, wallbangs
  • Kill feed with no-scopes, smoke shots, blind and airborne kills

Destruction & physics

  • Shatter glass, shoot out lights, burst pipes
  • Holes in thin walls; floors, stairs and roofs collapse
  • Real ragdolls, wounds and splatter (blood style RED or INK)
  • Trees fall and become cover

Weapons & fun

  • 13 guns from pistol to minigun, plus knife, katana and baseball bat
  • Hand-tuned draw, reload and inspect animations
  • FUN MODES: ONLY KNIFE / UTILITY / PISTOL / SNIPER / BAT
  • 10-mission campaign and 3 arenas against enemies that flank and call for help
